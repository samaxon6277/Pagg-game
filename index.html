💡 How to Play

Mathematical Point-Reflection Puzzle Rules

1

Point Reflection Formula

When Peg A (x_A, y_A) jumps over Pivot Peg B (x_B, y_B), it lands exactly at:

Destination C = (2x_B - x_A, 2y_B - y_A)
2

No Peg Elimination

Unlike classic Checkers or Solitaire, jumped pegs do not disappear! They remain on the board as pivotal reflection stepping stones for future jumps.

3

Goal Objective

Move ANY peg onto the glowing green laser energy pillar target hole in as few moves as possible to win!
